Problems with AI workflows

PR review overload

2×review workload per reviewer in one enterprise study as AI adoption grew

AI generates changes faster than teams can review them. The pressure lands on the people who check what ships.

Comprehension debt

59%of surveyed developers used AI code they did not fully understand

Delegating unfamiliar tasks leaves developers with working code they cannot explain or debug.

Lost in the codebase

You know what needs to change but not where to start. In an unfamiliar codebase, finding the relevant files and tracing how they connect can take longer than writing the change.

Onboarding a new hire costs effort, time and money. Developers start working effectively only once they have an overall picture of the system.
