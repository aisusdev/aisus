### CLD-18 Team page (2026-10-27)

The owner picked P-6, the last item of I9. `/<org>/team` lists the people who have signed in to aisus with access to the organization's repositories, each with their highest role there. It shows the seats bought and the seats in use, links to the organization's people on GitHub to invite or remove anyone, and gives an owner Manage seats, which opens Stripe's Customer Portal. It shows nothing anyone did. No approved design covers it, so it follows the settings and dashboard pages.

|---|---|---|---|
| `apps/cloud/src/lib/team.ts` (new) | `teamOf(db, account)` answers each person with access to the account's repositories, with login, name and highest role, sorted by role then login. It also answers the seats bought from `account_plans` and the seats in use from `seatsOf` | +55 | +41 |
| `apps/cloud/src/app/[owner]/team/page.tsx` (new) | Signed-out visitors go to sign in. A visitor without access to any of the account's repositories sees a short note. Tests:
- `[unit]` `teamOf` lists each person once with their highest role across the account's repositories, leaves out people with access only to other accounts, and answers the seats bought and in use.
- `[web]` An owner of a subscribed organization sees the people with their roles, the seats line, the GitHub people link and Manage seats. A viewer sees the same page without Manage seats.

Where the build differs: the seats in use are the people listed rather than `seatsOf`, whose floor of one seat is for buying. Stripe's Checkout webhook stores no seat count, so until `customer.subscription.updated` reports one the page shows only the seats in use. The dashboard link appears in both of the dashboard's views. The web test needed two new accounts in the GitHub mock, `guild` as admin and `crew` with read access only, and a `subscribe` helper in `test/web/support.ts` that signs a Checkout webhook. The webhook secret moved from `playwright.config.ts` into that file. The unit test is 42 lines, and the web test 32 lines with +18 / -7 in its helpers.
