4.1 Chen Dan Quirk divide and conquer

We split the work by time, not by area. Competitive programmers know CDQ divide and conquer, which cuts a problem along its time axis and solves each half in turn. Ours is the Chen Dan Quirk edition.

One of us holds the whole product at a time, in a shift of a few days. That person owns everything: the extension, the cloud, the server and the docs. They push aisus as far as they can with tunnel vision, write a handover, and pass it on. The next person starts from that handover and never waits on anyone. No one owns a module. Each of us answers for the whole product.
