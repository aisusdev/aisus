# Code of conduct

aisus is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents aisus.

## What we expect

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience, identity or background.
- Criticise the work, never the person. Explain what you would change and why.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Help newcomers find their way, as the project's docs try to.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on a change, and keep any disagreement to the work.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or remarks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Email aisus.dev@gmail.com if someone breaks this code. Only the maintainers read that address, and reports are handled in confidence.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide on a response that fits what happened. It can be a private word, a public warning, a removed comment, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
