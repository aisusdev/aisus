Things to try

  • Hover aisus: 3 changed in the status bar, then click it.

  • Ask tab: Clear, then pick a starter. It fills the box and sends nothing.
  • The map's ⋯ menu: Ask About This Map.
  • Source Control in the Activity Bar: the Review Changes icon.
